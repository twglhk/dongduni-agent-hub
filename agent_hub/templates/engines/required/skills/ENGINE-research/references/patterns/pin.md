# Research note · Pin the question — v1

## Status

draft

## Sources

Riot's balance articles on measuring before a playtest; any postmortem that starts from a number.

## Recipe

1. Write the question as a gap: what our number is, how it was measured (path, N, spread), the band it should reach.
2. If the brief lacks a measurement, take one with a read-only copy of the probe; never edit the product.

## Checks

- Is the gap quoted with a path and a sample size?
- Is the target band named and not just "better"?
- Is it one unit on both sides (game time vs wall clock, ticks vs seconds)?
