# Process: Research note — v1

Status: draft

A question that blocks a decision, answered from the best sources in the field and the project's own numbers. Edit the stages to your project, then confirm the card once a few notes have run it.

| # | Stage | Input | Output | Gate | Passed by | Pattern card |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pin the question to our numbers | `brief question` | `one paragraph: our measure, N, the band to reach` | the gap is quoted with its path and sample size; the target band is named | lane | patterns/pin.md |
| 2 | Read what we already have, then go outside | `paragraph` | `list of known facts; numbered findings with sources` | no outside lookup repeats a known fact; every finding has a source line and a tier | lane | patterns/sources.md |
| 3 | Band table | `findings` | `table: measure, best-in-class range, source, ours, gap` | both sides use one unit and one way of measuring; every row has a source | lane | patterns/bands.md |
| 4 | Gaps, gate lines, verdict | `band table` | `note file`: draft gate lines, ranked gaps with an owner, a verdict | each gap has an owner and a target a later check can pass or fail | lane, then hub | patterns/verdict.md |
